Famous Astronauts

Neil Armstrong

Neil Armstrong was an American astronaut and aerospace engineer. He became the first person to walk on the Moon during the Apollo 11 mission in 1969.

Born: August 5, 1930

Mission: Apollo 11

Image: NASA

Buzz Aldrin

Buzz Aldrin is an American astronaut and engineer. He became the second person to walk on the Moon during the Apollo 11 mission.

Born: January 20, 1930

Mission: Apollo 11

Image: NASA

Michael Collins

Michael Collins was the command module pilot of Apollo 11. He remained in lunar orbit while Armstrong and Aldrin explored the Moon.

Born: October 31, 1930

Mission: Apollo 11

Image: NASA

Apollo 11

On July 20, 1969, Apollo 11 became the first crewed mission to land humans on the Moon. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin explored the lunar surface while Michael Collins remained in orbit.
